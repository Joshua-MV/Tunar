# Tunar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Tunar ("the app") is a tuner for guitar, bass and ukulele made by Joshua Varghese. This policy
explains what the app does with your information. In short: **nothing leaves your device.**

## Microphone

Tunar uses your microphone only while the tuner is listening (after you press the start
button), so it can hear the note your instrument is playing.

- The sound is analysed on your device, in real time, to work out its pitch.
- Audio is **never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere**.
- You can stop listening at any time with the same button, and you can turn off microphone
  access for Tunar in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.

## Settings stored on your device

Tunar remembers a few preferences so they are there next time you open it: your instrument and
tuning, any custom tunings you save, the reference pitch (A4) and whether the in-tune chime is on.
These are stored only on your device and are deleted if you uninstall the app.

## What Tunar does not do

- It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal
  information.
- It does not use accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.
- It does not connect to the internet to send or receive your data.
- It does not share any information with anyone.

## Children

Tunar does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
